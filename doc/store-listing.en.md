# FocusMask — Make every page yours.

[简体中文](store-listing.zh-CN.md) · **English**

Hide unwanted page elements and apply Gaussian blur to images, videos or entire pages.

- Pick, preview and save element hiding rules.
- Click immediately or hover to reveal individual images and HTML5 videos. Hover defaults to 250ms and reveal to 1000ms; both are configurable.
- Blur selected websites on every visit and briefly reveal the page on demand.
- Toggle page blur with a keyboard shortcut, without changing saved site settings.
- Local-only rules, without accounts, cloud services or browsing logs.
- Independent modes and reversible visual changes.
- Simplified Chinese and English, with automatic browser-language selection and a manual choice.

FocusMask changes the appearance of authorized regular webpages. It does not block network requests or provide password protection. Browser pages, special documents and complex cross-origin content may be unsupported. Early protection cannot guarantee zero content flashes on every loading path.
